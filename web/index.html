HOW IT WORKS

Every world in this game — geometry and textures, 41 MB — lives in one compressed archive inside this page. Nothing is loaded at start except the Station. When you approach a place, the Archive restores only the chunks you need, verifies each one against its sha256 (this check is done by the game, not by the decoders), and the GPU assembles them out of light. Chunks you leave behind fold away.

The same chunks are packed three ways so you can switch the decoder at any time (M): ACEAPEX (fast profile, read by region), zstd 1.5.7 level 19 and LZ4HC 1.10 level 12. Restore cards and the benchmark show real timings from your machine. They are trade-offs: in our measurements zstd made the smallest archive and ACEAPEX and LZ4HC restored fastest; your machine may differ.

Limits: a page opened from a file runs the decoders on one CPU core; browser timers are coarse (~0.1 ms), so the benchmark repeats each test. The worlds are procedural, not production game assets.
